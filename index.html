Macroscopic Quantum Optics

We use experimental quantum optics in discrete and continuous variables to study quantum phenomena in large-scale and complex systems, from correlated light–matter states in molecular microcavities to the motion of levitated masses.

The group is led by Anton Zasedatelev and is part of the Department of Applied Physics at Aalto University.

Research

Correlated quantum states of light and matter

Quantum correlations from electronic and vibrational degrees of freedom coupled to light in solid-state systems and molecules.

Nonequilibrium Bose–Einstein condensates

Light–matter condensates in optical cavities as open quantum systems, and the devices they make possible.

Quantum control over large mass motion

Hybrid RF and dark optical trapping under ultra-high vacuum, to bring macroscopic particles into the quantum regime.
